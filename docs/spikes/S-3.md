# S-3 — MIDI/audio alignment (PENDING: physical devices) — the highest-consequence spike

**Threshold (go):** < 10 ms cumulative divergence between MIDI note frames and audio transients at 5 minutes.

**Blocks:** Voice+Piano as one take (§8.3). The clock strategy (`ClockMapping`) is implemented and JVM-tested against a
simulated ±200 ppm drift, but only hardware answers whether `AudioTimestamp` anchors behave on the target set.

## Protocol

1. With the S-2 probe, play a metronomic pattern (one key every 500 ms from a mechanical actuator or a second device
   triggering touches) for 5 minutes while recording Voice+Piano.
2. Export; load `performance.mid` and the WAV in a DAW; measure the offset of each MIDI Note On against the nearest audio
   transient (the sampler's click is recorded acoustically or via loopback).
3. Report offset at 0 s, 60 s, 180 s, 300 s; slope; and `ClockMapping` diagnostics from logcat (anchor count, ppm, clamp hits).

**Fallback if no-go:** anchor more often (every 250 ms), or drive the MIDI clock from the *output* stream's timestamps
instead of the input's. If neither closes the gap, Voice+Piano ships as two linked takes (a data-model change: raise it).
